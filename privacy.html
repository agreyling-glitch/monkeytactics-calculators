1. Who We Are

MonkeyTactics (monkeytactics.com) is a free online tool and calculator site. References to "we", "us", or "our" throughout this policy refer to MonkeyTactics and its operator.

If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us via the contact page.

2. Calculator & Tool Data

All calculations on MonkeyTactics run entirely within your browser using client-side JavaScript. This means:

  • Numbers, values, and inputs you enter are never transmitted to any server.
  • Results are computed locally on your device and displayed instantly.
  • When you close or navigate away from a tool page, any entered data is gone — we do not store it.

Your financial figures, health metrics, and any other inputs are yours alone.

3. Cloudflare CDN & Security

Our site is served through Cloudflare, which may collect technical information such as IP addresses, device details, and security-related data to provide performance optimization and protection against malicious traffic. This data is processed by Cloudflare according to its own Privacy Policy.

4. Cookies

MonkeyTactics itself does not set any first-party cookies for tracking, personalisation, or advertising purposes.

Third-party services embedded on the site (such as Google AdSense, described below) may set their own cookies in accordance with their own privacy policies. You can control or disable these through your browser's cookie settings.

5. Advertising

MonkeyTactics displays advertisements served by Google AdSense to support the cost of running the site and keeping all tools free.

Google AdSense may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings, or by using the NAI opt-out tool.

For more information on how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see Google's Privacy & Terms.

6. Third-Party Links

Some pages on MonkeyTactics may contain links to external websites for reference or context. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external sites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party site you visit.

7. Children's Privacy

MonkeyTactics is a general-purpose tool site and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this site, please contact us so we can address it.

8. Your Rights

Because MonkeyTactics does not collect personally identifiable information, there is generally no personal data for us to retrieve, correct, or delete on your behalf. However, depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable privacy laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK) or CCPA (California).

If you have any privacy-related questions or requests, please reach out via the contact page and we will respond as promptly as possible.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the site, applicable law, or our practices. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of MonkeyTactics after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed.
