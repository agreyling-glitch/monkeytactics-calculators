How to use the QR Code Generator

Choose your content type — a URL or plain text, or Wi-Fi login details. Type or paste the content into the field on the left. The preview on the right updates live as you type, so you can fine-tune the text before downloading.

When it looks right, click Generate QR Code to lock the preview in place, then use Download PNG for screen and social use, or Download SVG for crisp printing at any size.

What can you encode?

  • URLs — open a page, product, or contact form instantly when scanned.
  • Plain text — share notes, promo codes, or short messages.
  • Wi-Fi credentials — let guests join your network by scanning a code.

For Wi-Fi codes, the generator builds a standard payload that most modern phone cameras recognise automatically:

WIFI:S:NetworkName;T:WPA;P:Password;H:false;

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Are QR codes generated on this page free?

    Yes — the generator is completely free, requires no sign-up, and you can create and download as many codes as you need. Everything runs in your browser, so there are no queues or daily limits.

  • Is my data sent to a server?

    No. The text, URLs, and Wi-Fi credentials you enter never leave your browser — the QR image is generated locally with JavaScript. Nothing is uploaded or stored anywhere. See our Privacy Policy for details.

  • What is the difference between PNG and SVG?

    PNG is a raster image best for screens, social posts, and documents at a fixed size. SVG is a vector format that scales crisply to any size, making it ideal for large-format printing, signage, and embedding in other graphics.

  • Why won't my Wi-Fi QR code scan?

    Check that the encryption type matches your router (WPA/WPA2 is most common), include the hidden-network flag only if your network is actually hidden, and avoid special characters in the SSID or password that some phone cameras parse inconsistently. If a scan still fails, try regenerating after a small edit to the field.

  • Can I add a logo or change the colors?

    Not yet. Codes are generated with our brand green on white for high contrast and reliable scanning on every phone camera. Logos and custom colors reduce scannability unless error-correction is carefully tuned, so we keep the default high-contrast look.
