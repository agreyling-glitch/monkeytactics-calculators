Date difference calculator: business days and calendar dates

This free date difference calculator handles more than a basic day count. Use it to find an exact calendar difference, count elapsed calendar days, calculate business days with holiday exclusions, plan a buffered deadline, or generate a recurring date schedule.

  1. Choose Date Difference, Work Planning, or Recurring Dates.
  2. Enter the dates and select the calculation options that match your situation.
  3. Calculate the result, then copy its bookmarkable link or use the available print and download tools.

Choose the right date-difference precision

Exact years, months, and days

Exact mode gives a calendar-aware breakdown instead of treating every month or year as a fixed number of days. It is useful for calculating age, time since an event, contract length, anniversaries, and other spans normally described in years, months, and days.

Total calendar days

Total calendar days measures the elapsed number of days from one date to the other, including weekdays, weekends, and holidays. If the dates are entered in reverse order, the calculator still returns the size of the interval and identifies that the end date comes before the start date. A deadline indicator also compares the later date with today and labels it as past, high, medium, or low risk.

Business days

Business-day mode counts eligible workdays and skips Saturdays and Sundays. You can also exclude holidays by selecting the United States or European Union calendar, or by using a custom holiday list. Eligible start and end dates are included in the business-day total.

Hours and minutes

Use the hours/minutes option when a smaller unit is more useful than a calendar breakdown. The result includes total hours, total minutes, and the equivalent total days.

Count working days with weekends and holidays excluded

The business-day calculator evaluates each date in the selected range and keeps only dates that qualify as workdays:

Business Days = Eligible Weekdays − Selected Holidays

Choose None to skip weekends only. The built-in US calendar includes common federal holidays, while the EU option includes a common set of European public holidays. Holiday observance can vary by country, state, employer, or organization, so use Custom List when an exact local schedule matters.

Custom holidays may be typed directly or imported from a TXT or CSV file. Enter one date per line or separate dates with commas. The tool accepts up to 40 unique dates, requires the YYYY-MM-DD format, rejects impossible calendar dates, and reports duplicates before the calculation runs.

Plan project deadlines with business-day buffers

Work Planning mode combines a working-day count with deadline forecasting. Enter a project start date and target end date, choose the appropriate holiday calendar, and add optional buffer days for reviews, approvals, shipping delays, or other schedule risk.

Buffer days are added as business days, so weekends and selected holidays do not consume the buffer. Results show the business-day total, total calendar days, and buffered deadline. A mini timeline separates workdays, weekends, holidays, and buffer time, while making the full schedule easier to scan.

Generate, print, and export recurring dates

Recurring Dates mode builds a schedule by repeatedly adding a fixed interval to a start date. The start date is occurrence number one. Choose an interval in days and generate up to 200 occurrences for billing cycles, maintenance plans, medication reminders, inspections, appointments, or follow-up schedules.

The Upcoming Occurrences table includes the sequence number, formatted date, weekday, and number of days from the start. Print a clean copy or download the schedule as CSV for spreadsheet use or as an aligned TXT file for notes and plain-text records.

Private calculations and bookmarkable results

Calculations, custom holiday validation, and file imports run in your browser; no account or sign-up is required. Copy bookmarkable link creates a URL containing the current configuration so you can save or share a calculation. Because that URL contains the selected settings, review it before sharing dates or a custom holiday list.

Date Difference Calculator FAQs

  • How do I calculate the exact number of years, months, and days between two dates?

    Choose Date Difference, enter the start and end dates, select Exact (years, months, days), and calculate. This calendar-aware result is useful for ages, anniversaries, contracts, and other spans normally described in years, months, and days.

  • What is the difference between calendar days and business days?

    Calendar days include every date in the elapsed span. Business days include eligible weekdays and exclude Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays from the selected calendar. Business-day totals include eligible start and end dates.

  • Are the start and end dates included?

    Exact, total-day, hour, and minute results measure elapsed time between the dates. Business Day and Work Planning modes count both the start and end dates when they are eligible workdays. Recurring Dates includes the start date as occurrence number one.

  • Can I exclude holidays or upload a custom holiday list?

    Yes. Select the United States or European Union calendar, choose None to skip weekends only, or use Custom List. You can type dates or import a TXT or CSV file containing up to 40 unique, valid dates in YYYY-MM-DD format.

  • What does Work Planning mode calculate?

    Work Planning counts business days through a target date, adds an optional business-day buffer, estimates the buffered deadline, and displays a timeline that distinguishes workdays, weekends, holidays, and buffer time.

  • Can I print or download recurring dates?

    Yes. Generate the recurring schedule, then print the Upcoming Occurrences table or download it as CSV or aligned plain text. Each export includes the occurrence number, ISO date, weekday, and days from the start.

  • Are my dates private, and can I share a calculation?

    Calculations and file imports are processed in your browser. The calculator does not require an account. If you use Copy bookmarkable link, the current settings are placed in the URL so you can save or share that configuration. See our Privacy Policy for details.
