      How to decode a QR code or barcode

      Choose Auto detect, QR only, or Barcode only, then upload an image, paste a screenshot, enter a public image URL, or scan with your camera. The decoder returns up to 10 distinct codes from one image or camera frame. For the most reliable results, keep every code complete, sharp, and surrounded by a clear border.

      Local QR image scanning with private link resolution

      This free QR code reader performs decoding locally with Rust/WebAssembly, the browser Barcode Detector when available, and a local ZXing/WebAssembly fallback. Uploaded files, pasted screenshots, and camera frames are not uploaded to MonkeyTactics or stored by this utility.

      When a scan returns one website address, that decoded link is sent to the MonkeyTactics privacy proxy to show each redirect hop, HTTP status, timing, and final destination for your review. Links in multi-code results remain unchecked until you choose an action. The proxy uses header-only requests; it does not send the QR image, read page content, or automatically open the destination in your browser. You can optionally check a link using VirusTotal before visiting it.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      • How do I decode a QR code from an image?

        Upload or drag an image into the decoder, paste a screenshot, or enter a public image URL. The tool scans the image locally and displays up to 10 detected QR codes and barcodes together.

      • Can I scan a QR code with my computer camera?

        Yes. Choose Auto detect, QR only, or Barcode only, allow camera access, and keep one or more complete codes visible in the mode-aware camera guide. The decoder captures up to 10 detected codes from the frame. Camera access requires HTTPS or a localhost address and may be disabled by managed browser policies.

      • Which QR code and barcode formats are supported?

        The supported-formats table above lists every available QR and barcode format. Auto mode checks both columns; QR only and Barcode only restrict detection to their corresponding column. The detected format appears with the result.

      • Are my QR code images uploaded or stored?

        No. Uploaded, pasted, and camera images are decoded locally in your browser. They are not sent to MonkeyTactics or stored by this tool.

      • Why can’t the decoder find my QR code?

        The image may be blurry, cropped, too dark, distorted, or too low resolution. Try a sharper image that shows the entire code and the light-colored border around it.

      • Why won’t an image URL load?

        The remote image host must permit cross-origin browser access (CORS). If it does not, save the image to your device and use the upload option instead.

      • How can I check whether a decoded QR link is safe?

        Decoded web links are sent to the MonkeyTactics privacy proxy only to resolve their final destination for your review; they are never opened automatically. Select Check Link Safely to open the final URL's VirusTotal analysis page. Review unfamiliar destinations before visiting them or entering personal information.

      • What image formats does the QR decoder support?

        It supports common formats your browser can read, including PNG, JPEG, GIF, and WebP. PNG or a high-quality JPEG usually produces the most reliable scan.
