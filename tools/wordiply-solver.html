    From Starter to Longest Word

    1. Choose a dictionary: use ENABLE for a broad North American list, SOWPODS for British and North American vocabulary, or Both for the widest combined results. Hover over or focus an option for details. SOWPODS is the recommended starting point for Wordiply.
    2. Copy the starter letters from today’s Wordiply.

    4. Start with the top results: ties are grouped by length and sorted alphabetically.

    03 / Understand the game

    What Is Wordiply?

    Wordiply is a daily word game in which you get five attempts to make long words containing a short starter. The starter must remain together and in the same order, but you can add letters before it, after it, or on both sides.

    The game reports a length score based on how close your best entry is to the longest available word and a letter score based on the total letters used across your attempts. This solver focuses on finding long candidates; it does not predict Wordiply’s private accepted-word list.

    Read the rules or play the daily puzzle on the official Wordiply game.

    Practical strategy

    How to Improve Your Wordiply Score

    • Search with the starter anywhere before testing prefix-only or suffix-only ideas.
    • Try productive endings such as -ing, -ation, -able, and -ness when they form real words.
    • Look for a prefix and a suffix together; extending both sides often produces the longest result.
    • Use several strong entries if you also want to improve the total letter score.

    04 / Word coverage

    ENABLE, SOWPODS, and Wordiply Answers

    ENABLE is a broad public-domain English word list commonly used for North American word games. SOWPODS combines British and North American word-game vocabulary, making it the more useful starting point for a British game such as Wordiply. Choose Both for maximum coverage with duplicate words removed.

    These lists are independent of Wordiply. They can contain obscure words that the live game rejects and may omit newer or specially selected answers that Wordiply accepts. Always confirm a suggestion in the game. See the dictionary sources and licenses.

    Method and privacy

    How the Solver Finds Matches

    The tool loads compressed dictionary data into a WebAssembly search engine in your browser. It wraps the normalized starter in flexible wildcards, keeps only words containing the exact sequence, applies your position and minimum-length choices, and sorts results from longest to shortest.

    Your starter, filters, and result list stay on your device. No search account is required and the solver does not send your entries to MonkeyTactics.

    05 / Common questions

    Wordiply Solver FAQ

    Does the starter have to stay together?

    Yes. It must appear as one uninterrupted sequence in the same order.

    Which dictionary should I choose?

    SOWPODS is the best starting point for British vocabulary. ENABLE is a broad North American list; Both gives the widest set of ideas.

    Will every result be accepted by Wordiply?

    No. This helper uses independent public word lists, not Wordiply’s private answer list. Treat results as suggestions and confirm them in the game.
