How to use the Tip Calculator

Enter three values: the bill amount (the pre-tip subtotal on your check), the tip percentage you want to leave, and the number of people splitting the bill. Use the sliders for quick adjustments, tap a preset chip for common tip percentages, or type an exact value into the number fields.

Click Calculate Tip to instantly see how much each person pays, the grand total including tip, and the tip amount on its own. The breakdown bar shows how much of the total is the bill versus the tip.

How is a tip calculated?

A tip is the bill multiplied by the tip percentage, expressed as a decimal:

Tip = Bill × ( Tip% ÷ 100 ) · Total = Bill + Tip · Per Person = Total ÷ People

For example, an 18% tip on an $85.50 bill: 85.50 × 0.18 = $15.39 tip, for a total of $100.89. Split between 4 people, that's $25.22 each.

For a quick mental shortcut: 10% is just the bill with the decimal shifted one place left, 20% is double that, and 15% is 10% plus half of 10%.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is the standard tip percentage?

    In the United States, the customary tip at a sit-down restaurant is 15–20% of the pre-tax bill. For excellent service, 20–25% is common. For counter service or takeout, 10% or a small flat amount is typical. Tipping customs vary widely by country — in much of Europe and Asia a service charge is already included in the bill.

  • How do I calculate a tip by hand?

    To calculate a 20% tip quickly: move the decimal point one place to the left (that's 10%), then double it. For a $45 bill, 10% is $4.50, so 20% is $9.00. For 15%, find 10% and add half of it: $4.50 + $2.25 = $6.75.

  • What if the split doesn't divide evenly?

    The calculator rounds each amount to the cent. In practice, one person may pay a few cents more or less to cover rounding — the per-person total shown is the mathematically even split.

  • Should I tip before or after tax?

    The most common practice is to tip on the pre-tax subtotal, since sales tax isn't part of the service you received. Some people tip on the post-tax total for simplicity. This calculator uses whichever bill amount you enter.

  • When should I tip more or less?

    Large groups often trigger an automatic 18–20% gratuity for parties of 6 or more — check your bill first. At a buffet, 10% is generally enough since you serve yourself. For delivery, 15–20% with a $3–$5 minimum is standard.

  • Is my data private?

    Yes — all calculations run entirely in your browser using JavaScript. No numbers you enter are transmitted to any server or stored anywhere. See our Privacy Policy for details.
