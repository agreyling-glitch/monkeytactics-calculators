        02 / Use the solver

        How to Solve Absurdle

        1. Choose a dictionary: use ENABLE for a broad, public-domain North American list, SOWPODS for British and North American vocabulary, or Both for the widest search. Hover over or focus an option for more detail.
        2. Enter your completed five-letter guess.
        3. Click each tile to match Absurdle’s gray, yellow, and green feedback.
        4. Select Add feedback & analyze.
        5. Review the progression chart, play the recommended word, then repeat with the response you receive.

        The key difference

        How the Adversary Works

        Absurdle does not commit to one answer at the start. It groups every remaining word by the feedback each could return, then keeps a large compatible group so the puzzle stays alive.

        This solver counters that strategy by minimizing the largest group the opponent can preserve. The “worst case” number is how many answers may remain after its most difficult reply.

        The search-space progression chart uses a logarithmic scale to show the actual remaining pool after each guess alongside the recommended guess’s projected worst case.

        03 / Understand the results

        Choose the Best Next Absurdle Guess

        The recommendation minimizes the largest group of answers Absurdle can keep after responding. A lower worst-case count is safer because even the game’s most evasive color pattern leaves fewer possible words.

        The ranked list also shows the number of possible replies and the average remaining pool. A suggested guess does not need to be a surviving answer if it separates the possibilities more effectively.

        Save progress locally

        Pause and Resume an Absurdle Game

        Every game with at least one submitted clue is saved automatically in Game history. Open it to restore the exact guesses, tile colors, and dictionary used for that session.

        Up to 25 games remain in this browser. The selected game has a gold outline, and you can delete one session or clear the complete history. Saved games never leave your device.

        04 / Common questions

        Absurdle Solver FAQ

        Is this a normal Wordle solver?

        No. A normal solver optimizes for one fixed answer; this one evaluates the adversarial answer pool Absurdle can retain.

        Are repeated letters supported?

        Yes. Feedback patterns are scored in two passes, so duplicate greens and yellows use the correct available letter counts.

        What does worst case mean?

        It is the size of the largest feedback partition for a guess—the reply that leaves you with the most possible answers.

        Why might a guess not be a possible answer?

        An exploratory word can split the remaining pool more effectively than guessing one of the survivors.

        Does it run privately?

        Yes. Dictionary filtering and adversarial scoring happen in your browser.

        Is it affiliated with Absurdle?

        No. This independent helper is not affiliated with or endorsed by Absurdle or its creators.
