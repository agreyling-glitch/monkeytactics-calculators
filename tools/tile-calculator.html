How to use the Tile Calculator

Enter the tiled area’s length and width in feet, choose a tile size, and set a waste percentage. Click Calculate Tile to see adjusted coverage, individual tiles, full boxes, and boxed weight.

A 10% waste allowance is common for straight layouts. Diagonal patterns, complex rooms, or fragile tile may require 15% or more.

How is tile quantity calculated?

The calculator increases the floor area by the selected waste percentage, divides by the coverage of one tile, and rounds up to whole tiles and boxes.

Adjusted area = length × width × (1 + waste ÷ 100)
Tiles = round up(adjusted area ÷ tile area)
Boxes = round up(tiles ÷ tiles per box)

Box quantities and weights are representative values for the selected ceramic tile size and should be checked against the actual product carton.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How much extra tile should I buy?

    Use about 10% extra for a simple straight layout and 15% or more for diagonal patterns, many corners, or future repair stock.

  • Why are boxes rounded up?

    Tile is usually sold only in full cartons. Rounding up ensures the order contains enough whole tiles after cuts and breakage.

  • Does the calculator include grout spacing?

    No. Grout joints slightly reduce tile coverage needs, but the waste allowance typically exceeds that difference for project estimating.
