How this estimate works

For a rectangular room, wall area is 2 × (length + width) × height. For individual walls or irregular rooms, choose total wall length and add the wall lengths yourself. Doors, windows, and other unpainted areas are deducted from walls only. Ceiling area is length × width, or your entered ceiling area.

Paint gallons = net surface area × coats × (1 + allowance ÷ 100) ÷ coverage
Primer gallons = selected net area × primer coats × (1 + allowance ÷ 100) ÷ primer coverage

Wall paint, ceiling paint, and primer are purchased separately. The shopping planner compares enabled quart, gallon, and five-gallon containers, rounds up to cover each product’s requirement, and reports leftover liquid. Sales tax applies to paint, primer, and entered supplies; labor and delivery are excluded. Primer does not automatically reduce finish coats.

Planning tips & common questions

  • Which measurements should I enter?

    Measure each room separately. Use total wall length for an irregular layout or a single wall. For openings, enter the combined area of doors and windows you will not paint. Different-height walls can be separate entries.

  • How much coverage should I use?

    The default 350 square feet per gallon is a starting assumption. Replace it with your paint label’s coverage and account for surface texture and application method. Ceiling paint and primer have independent coverage settings.

  • Are projects saved?

    Your latest valid project is saved in this browser when local storage is available. Room names and measurements remain on your device. CSV and print exports include the current estimate. Reset removes the saved project and starts fresh.

  • What is included in the budget?

    Selected paint and primer containers, entered supply quantities and prices, and your sales tax. Include brushes, rollers, tape, protective coverings, and repair materials in the supplies section. Delivery and labor are excluded. Prices, coverage, and liquid weights are editable planning assumptions.
