How to use the Paint Calculator

Add together the lengths of every wall you plan to paint and enter that total in feet. Enter the wall height and number of coats, then click Calculate Paint.

The result shows total coated square footage, exact calculated gallons, and the whole-gallon quantity to purchase. Total weight uses the rounded purchase quantity.

How is paint quantity calculated?

Wall area is multiplied by the number of coats, then divided by an assumed coverage rate of 350 square feet per gallon.

Coated area = wall length × wall height × coats
Gallons = coated area ÷ 350
Whole gallons to buy = round up(gallons)

Actual coverage depends on paint type, color change, surface porosity, texture, and application method.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How much area does one gallon of paint cover?

    A typical gallon of interior latex paint covers about 350 to 400 square feet for one coat. This calculator uses 350 square feet for a conservative estimate.

  • Should I subtract doors and windows?

    You can subtract large openings, but leaving standard doors and windows in the area provides extra paint for touch-ups and coverage variation.

  • Do dark color changes require more paint?

    They can. Dramatic color changes, unprimed drywall, and porous surfaces may need primer or an additional coat beyond the estimate.
