How to use the Roofing Shingle Calculator

Enter the horizontal roof area in square feet and select the roof pitch. Click Calculate Shingles to estimate the sloped roof area, roofing squares, bundles, and total bundle weight.

The horizontal area is the footprint beneath the roof, not the already-sloped surface. The calculator adjusts that footprint for pitch and adds 10% waste.

How are roofing shingles calculated?

The selected pitch creates a slope factor. The horizontal area is multiplied by that factor and a 10% waste allowance before converting to roofing squares and bundles.

Pitch factor = √(12² + rise²) ÷ 12
Adjusted area = horizontal area × pitch factor × 1.10
Squares = adjusted area ÷ 100
Bundles = round up(squares × 3)

The estimate assumes three bundles cover one roofing square and each standard asphalt shingle bundle weighs 70 lb.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is one roofing square?

    One roofing square is the amount of roofing material needed to cover 100 square feet of roof surface.

  • How many shingle bundles make a square?

    Most standard asphalt shingles use three bundles per square. Some premium or specialty products require a different number.

  • Is 10% roof waste always enough?

    Simple gable roofs often use about 10%. Roofs with many valleys, hips, dormers, or complex cuts may need 15% or more.
