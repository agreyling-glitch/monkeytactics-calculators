Mortgage Calculator Questions

How is my mortgage payment calculated?

A fixed-rate mortgage payment is calculated from the loan principal, periodic interest rate, and total number of payments. This report estimates principal and interest only; taxes, insurance, escrow, and fees are not included.

What is an amortization schedule?

An amortization schedule lists each loan payment and shows how much goes toward interest, how much reduces principal, and the remaining mortgage balance after the payment.

How do extra payments affect my mortgage?

Extra payments reduce principal sooner. In the model, this lowers future interest charges and can shorten the payoff period. Confirm prepayment rules and processing details with your lender.

How it works

How is my mortgage payment calculated?

A fixed-rate payment is calculated from principal, the interest rate per payment period, and the number of payments. The scheduled principal-and-interest payment stays level, while its composition changes.

M = P × [r(1 + r)n] ÷ [(1 + r)n − 1]

Early payments tend to contain more interest. As the balance falls, more of the same payment reaches principal.

Questions, answered

Frequently Asked Questions

How is my mortgage payment calculated?

The calculator uses the standard fixed-rate amortization formula. It includes principal and interest, but not taxes, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance, HOA dues, or lender fees.

What is an amortization schedule?

An amortization schedule lists each payment and shows how it divides between interest and principal, along with the remaining balance after that payment.

How do extra payments affect my mortgage?

Extra money is applied to principal in the model. That lowers the balance sooner, reduces future interest, and can shorten the payoff time. Confirm prepayment rules with your lender.

What does bi-weekly mean here?

The bi-weekly model uses 26 equal payment periods per year and accrues interest for each of those periods. Lender programs can process bi-weekly payments differently, so compare the estimate with your loan terms.

Is this financial advice?

No. Results are educational estimates. Rates, rounding, payment timing, escrow, fees, and lender rules can change real loan costs.

Methodology & limitations

Fixed-rate, fully amortizing model. Results are rounded to cents for display; internal calculations use floating-point precision. Confirm all figures with a lender. Prepared and reviewed by MonkeyTactics. Last reviewed August 6, 2026.
