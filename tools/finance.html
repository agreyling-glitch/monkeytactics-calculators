Frequently Asked Questions

  • How accurate is the mortgage calculator?

    The loan and mortgage calculator uses standard amortization formulas identical to those used by banks. Results are accurate for fixed-rate loans. For variable-rate or interest-only mortgages, treat the output as an estimate and consult your lender for exact figures.

  • Does the compound interest calculator account for taxes?

    Yes — the compound interest calculator includes optional fields for tax rate and inflation, allowing you to model real after-tax returns and the true purchasing power of your savings over time.

  • Is my financial data stored or shared?

    No. All calculations happen entirely in your browser using JavaScript. No numbers you enter are transmitted to any server, stored in a database, or shared with any third party. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

  • Can I use these calculators on mobile?

    Yes — all MonkeyTactics tools are fully responsive and work on any device including smartphones and tablets, with no app download required.
