Frequently Asked Questions

  • How accurate is the mortgage calculator?

    The mortgage calculator uses a local Rust/WebAssembly engine and standard fixed-rate amortization formulas. It models monthly or bi-weekly schedules and optional extra principal payments. Estimates exclude taxes, insurance, escrow, fees, and lender-specific processing, so confirm final figures with your lender.

  • Does the compound interest calculator account for taxes?

    Yes — the compound interest calculator includes optional fields for tax rate and inflation, allowing you to model real after-tax returns and the true purchasing power of your savings over time.

  • Is my financial data stored or shared?

    No. Calculations happen entirely in your browser using local JavaScript and WebAssembly engines. No numbers you enter are transmitted to any server, stored in a database, or shared with any third party. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

  • Can I use these calculators on mobile?

    Yes — all MonkeyTactics tools are fully responsive and work on any device including smartphones and tablets, with no app download required.
