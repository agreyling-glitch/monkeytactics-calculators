Privacy-first utility tools

All seven tools on this page run entirely within your browser. Whether you're entering a bill amount, a Wi-Fi password, or travel dates — none of that information is ever sent to a server or stored in any database. The tools work offline once the page has loaded.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What unit types does the Unit Converter support?

    The unit converter handles length, weight, temperature, volume, area, and speed — covering the most common metric-to-imperial and imperial-to-metric conversions needed in everyday life.

  • Can I download the QR code I generate?

    Yes — the QR Code Generator lets you download your code as a PNG image, ready to use in documents, printed materials, or digital assets.

  • Does the Time Zone Converter adjust for daylight saving time?

    Yes — the Time Zone Converter accounts for daylight saving time rules for all supported regions, so the converted times are accurate year-round.

  • Can the Date Difference Calculator count working days only?

    The calculator returns the total calendar days between two dates. A working-days mode (excluding weekends and public holidays) is on our roadmap. Use the contact page to vote for it.
