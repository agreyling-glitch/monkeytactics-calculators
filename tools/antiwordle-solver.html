      How to Use the Antiwordle Solver

      1. Choose a dictionary: use ENABLE for a broad North American list, EXPANDED for British and North American vocabulary, or Both for the widest combined results. Hover over or focus an option for details.
      2. Enter the five-letter word you played.
      3. Click each tile to mark it gray, yellow, or red. Unmarked tiles count as gray.
      4. Select Add feedback & find safe guesses.
      5. Choose a legal suggestion, play it in Antiwordle, and add the new feedback.
      6. Edit or remove the newest row if a color was entered incorrectly.

      Mandatory rules

      What Makes a Guess Legal?

      Gray letters cannot be reused. Yellow letters must appear again but cannot stay in the same position. Red letters are locked and must remain in that square.

      The solver also accounts for repeated letters. If one copy is yellow or red and another is gray, later guesses use the confirmed number of copies without adding an eliminated extra copy.

      03 / Tile meanings

      Red, Yellow, and Gray Explained

      Red letter
      The hidden answer contains this letter in this exact position. Every later guess must keep it there.
      Yellow letter
      The letter is required, but every later guess must move it away from this position.
      Gray letter
      The letter is eliminated, except when another copy in the same row establishes a required count.

      Avoiding the answer

      How Safe Guesses Are Ranked

      Every legal Antiwordle guess remains a possible hidden answer when guesses and answers use the same dictionary. The helper ranks lower-information choices first because they are expected to preserve more uncertainty.

      A recommendation reduces information gain but cannot guarantee safety. The displayed uniform risk is one divided by the number of remaining answers.

      04 / Common questions

      Antiwordle Solver FAQ

      Does the solver enforce Antiwordle rules?

      Yes. Suggestions exclude eliminated gray letters, include required yellow letters in new positions, and preserve red letters in their locked positions.

      Can any suggestion still be the answer?

      Yes. Every legal guess remains consistent with the feedback when the same dictionary supplies guesses and answers. The solver reduces information gain; it cannot guarantee a safe word.

      Is this Antiwordle helper free?

      Yes. The solver is free to use, requires no account, and runs locally in your browser.

      What does the avoidance score mean?

      A lower score indicates a guess expected to reveal less information and preserve more uncertainty about the hidden answer.

      Are repeated letters supported?

      Yes. Mixed feedback for duplicate letters establishes minimum and maximum copy counts for later guesses.

      Are my guesses private?

      Yes. Dictionary filtering and ranking run locally in your browser.
