    How to Use the Crossword Solver

    1. Count the answer squares. Enter each known letter in its position and use ? for every blank.
    2. Select ENABLE for a broad general-English list, SOWPODS for international word-game vocabulary, or Both.
    3. Set Word Length when your pattern uses *, or when you want an extra check against the grid.
    4. Add Starts With, Ends With, Must Include, or Exclude Letters only when the clue or crossing answers support them.
    5. Open a result to check its definition against the clue.

    Crossword Solver FAQ

    How do I solve a crossword clue with this?

    Combine the clue with the letter positions supplied by crossing answers. Enter known letters exactly where they appear and use ? for each empty square. Then compare the matching words with the clue's meaning, tense, and wordplay.

    How do I search by pattern only?

    Enter the pattern and leave Available Letters empty. Unlike an unscrambler, this solver does not enforce rack-letter counts unless you deliberately provide a letter pool.

    Can I limit results to a specific length?

    Yes. Patterns without * already have a fixed length. You can also set Word Length, which is especially useful with flexible patterns such as B*.

    What is the difference between ? and *?

    A question mark matches exactly one letter—the equivalent of one empty grid square. An asterisk matches zero or more letters, so it is useful when you know an edge or fragment but not the total length.

    Which dictionary should I choose?

    ENABLE is a broad public-domain English word list suited to general searches. SOWPODS includes international word-game vocabulary. Both searches their deduplicated union, which is larger and may include obscure entries.

    Are my searches private?

    Yes. The dictionaries and matching engine run entirely in your browser. Your pattern and filters are not sent to a search service.
