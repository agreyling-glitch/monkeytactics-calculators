Free password, passphrase and username generator

This all-in-one security utility creates strong random passwords, XKCD-style passphrases, usernames and unique email aliases. Select the Password, Passphrase or Username tab, choose your options and generate a result instantly. Generation happens locally in your browser, so the secret itself is not submitted to MonkeyTactics.

Customize and generate secure passwords

Choose a password length from 4 to 128 characters and include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and symbols. The Minimum Numbers and Minimum Special Characters sliders guarantee the amount required by a website's password rules.

Open Advanced Options to remove similar characters such as I, l, 1, O and 0; prevent duplicate characters; insert a memorable word; or make every password begin with a chosen prefix. The quantity slider can generate 1 to 30 passwords. Multiple results appear in a table that you can copy, print or download as a plain-text TXT file.

Create memorable XKCD-style passphrases

The passphrase generator combines 3 to 12 unrelated words from curated dictionary data. Choose a hyphen, period, underscore, space or no separator; capitalize each word; and optionally add a random number. Proper nouns are excluded, while filters for offensive words and extremely obscure dictionary terms can be switched on or off. The result includes an estimated entropy score to help compare passphrase strength.

Generate usernames and unique email aliases

Create a random word username with optional capitalization and a number. You can also enter an existing email to make a plus-addressed email alias, such as name+tag@example.com, or enter a domain with catch-all email enabled to create a unique address. Email aliases can help organize sign-ups and identify which service received an address, but support for plus addressing depends on your email provider.

Why use a QR code for a password?

Select QR Code to turn a password, passphrase, username, email alias or password list into a high-contrast black-on-white QR code. A QR code is useful when moving a long password to another trusted device: scanning is faster than manual entry, preserves the exact capitalization, symbols and character order, and avoids mistakes caused by confusing or mistyped characters. It can also transfer a secret without placing it in a synced clipboard.

A QR code is a visual copy of the underlying text, not encryption. Anyone who can see or scan it can recover the password. Only display it around people and devices you trust, avoid saving or sharing screenshots, and close the page when the transfer is complete. For long-term storage and autofill, use a reputable password manager.

What makes a generated password strong?

The generator uses your browser's built-in crypto.getRandomValues() API to choose characters with cryptographically secure randomness, followed by a Fisher-Yates shuffle. Longer passwords drawn from a larger character pool are generally harder to brute-force. The strength meter estimates entropy in bits:

Entropy (bits) = Length × log₂(Character Pool Size)

Use a unique password for every account. Avoid reusing generated passwords or adding predictable personal details, and store important credentials in a password manager.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Is my generated password stored or sent anywhere?

    No — this tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. No password you generate is ever transmitted to a server, logged, or stored anywhere. See our Privacy Policy for details.

  • Why generate a QR code for a password?

    A QR code can move a long, complex password to another trusted device without retyping it. Scanning preserves uppercase letters, numbers, symbols and exact character order, reducing transcription errors. It can also avoid placing the password in a clipboard that syncs between devices.

  • Is it safe to share a password QR code?

    No. Treat the QR code exactly like the password itself because anyone who can view or scan it can read the secret. Use it only on trusted devices, do not post or save the image, and close the page after transferring the password.

  • What password options can I customize?

    Choose a length from 4 to 128 characters, minimum numbers and special characters, uppercase and lowercase letters, digits and symbols. Advanced options can remove similar or duplicate characters, add a word or prefix, and generate up to 30 passwords at once for copying, printing or TXT download.

  • How does the XKCD-style passphrase generator work?

    It combines 3 to 12 unrelated dictionary words using your chosen separator. You can capitalize words, include a number, and filter offensive or extremely obscure words. Proper nouns are excluded from the available word data.

  • What can the username generator create?

    It can create a random word username with optional capitalization and numbers, a plus-addressed alias for an existing email address, or a unique address for a domain that has catch-all email enabled.
