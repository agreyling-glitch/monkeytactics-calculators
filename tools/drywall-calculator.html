How to use the Drywall Calculator

Enter the room length, width, and wall height in feet. Click Calculate Drywall to estimate the total wall area and compare the number of 4×8 and 4×12 sheets.

The estimate covers all four walls. Doors and windows are not deducted, leaving a useful allowance for cuts and damaged pieces.

How is drywall quantity calculated?

The calculator finds the room perimeter, multiplies it by wall height, and divides the resulting wall area by the coverage of each sheet size.

Wall area = 2 × (length + width) × height
4×8 sheets = round up(wall area ÷ 32)
4×12 sheets = round up(wall area ÷ 48)

Total weight equals the rounded sheet count multiplied by 50 lb for a 4×8 sheet or 75 lb for a 4×12 sheet.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Does this drywall estimate include the ceiling?

    No. It calculates the four room walls only. For a flat ceiling, add length × width to the wall area before dividing by sheet coverage.

  • Should doors and windows be subtracted?

    Small openings are often left in the estimate to cover waste. For many large openings, subtract their combined area for a tighter estimate.

  • Why compare 4×8 and 4×12 sheets?

    Larger sheets can reduce seams but are heavier and harder to maneuver. The best size depends on room access, framing, and installer preference.
