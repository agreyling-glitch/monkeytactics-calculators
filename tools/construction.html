Plan construction materials with confidence

Accurate material estimates make it easier to set a realistic project budget, compare supplier quotes, and avoid delays caused by an extra trip to the store. These construction calculators cover common jobs including concrete slabs, drywall installation, painting, tile, roofing, lumber, and insulation.

Start with careful measurements of the area you are building or finishing. Use consistent units, double-check irregular shapes, and divide complex spaces into smaller rectangles when necessary. Small measurement errors can become significant when they are multiplied across a large project.

Most projects also need a waste allowance for cuts, breakage, spillage, damaged material, and future repairs. The tile and roofing calculators include waste in their estimates. The Paint Calculator lets you adjust an allowance for application losses and touch-ups after deducting doors and windows. Check each tool's assumptions against your project and product requirements.

Every calculator also estimates total material weight to support delivery and handling plans. Product dimensions, package coverage, and weight vary by manufacturer, so always confirm the calculator result against the product label, supplier specifications, vehicle payload limits, and applicable building codes before purchasing.

Budget for painting a room or multiple rooms

The Paint Calculator estimates wall paint, ceiling paint, and optional primer separately, then compares available quart, gallon, and five-gallon containers. Enter supply quantities and prices for masking tape, rollers, brushes, drop cloths, and repair materials to include them in your materials budget with sales tax.

Start with the Drywall Calculator for unfinished walls, then plan painting after the wall surfaces are ready. Paint projects can be measured in feet or meters and saved in your browser, exported as CSV, or printed as a shopping list. Labor and delivery are excluded from the painting estimate.
