How it works

Build exact phrase anagrams, not approximate matches

The solver accounts for every letter in the original text. Spaces, punctuation, and capitalization are ignored, but no letter can be added, removed, or reused.

  1. 01Count the lettersNormalize the source into an exact letter inventory.
  2. 02Explore combinationsSearch local dictionaries across parallel browser workers.
  3. 03Rank natural phrasesScore vocabulary, word order, and phrase shape.
  4. 04Verify every resultReject anything that is not an exact anagram.

Better results

How to use the advanced anagram filters

  • Phrase pattern: use ? for one unknown letter and * for any number.
  • Required: lock one or more words into every generated result.
  • Preferred: promote interesting vocabulary without making it mandatory.
  • Excluded: remove distracting words or families of unwanted results.
  • Search depth: start with Deep and use Exhaustive when breadth matters more than speed.
  • Dictionary: Expanded finds more unusual words but can introduce additional noise.
✓

Private, local anagram generation

Your source phrase, filters, generated results, and Pick List remain in your browser. The Rust/WebAssembly search engine loads local word data and does not send the text you enter to a remote solving service.

Questions

Anagram solver FAQ

What is a phrase anagram?

A phrase anagram rearranges every letter from a name or phrase into new words. Spaces, punctuation, and capitalization do not affect the match.

Are all results exact?

Yes. Each displayed phrase is validated against the source and must contain exactly the same letters.

Why do some searches take longer?

Long phrases, larger dictionaries, short minimum word lengths, and exhaustive depth create far more combinations to explore.

How do I find a specific phrase?

Add its known structure to Phrase pattern, lock confirmed words as Required, or mark promising vocabulary as Preferred.

What does the vulgar-word filter do?

It removes clearly explicit and profane dictionary entries before searching. It can be disabled under Advanced options.

Where is my Pick List stored?

The Pick List is saved locally in this browser so it can remain available across searches.
