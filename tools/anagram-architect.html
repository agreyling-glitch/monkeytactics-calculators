How it works

Build exact phrase anagrams, not approximate matches

The solver accounts for every letter in the original text. Spaces, punctuation, and capitalization are ignored, but no letter can be added, removed, or reused.

  1. 01Count every letterNormalize the source into an exact letter inventory.
  2. 02Guide the searchApply grammar templates, phrase patterns, and vocabulary preferences when you know part of the answer.
  3. 03Explore and rankSearch local dictionaries across parallel workers, then rank exact phrases for readability and natural word order.
  4. 04Refine your favoritesSave results to the Pick List to arrange words, replace a weak word, and style the finished phrase.

How to use the advanced anagram filters

  • Phrase pattern: use ? for one unknown letter and * for any number.
  • Grammar template: request a noun, verb, or adjective structure; connector words are reserved automatically.
  • Required: include one or more confirmed words in every generated result.
  • Preferred: promote interesting vocabulary without making it mandatory.
  • Excluded: remove distracting words or families of unwanted results.
  • Search depth: start with Deep and use Exhaustive when breadth matters more than speed.
  • Dictionary: Expanded finds more unusual words but can introduce additional noise.
  • Pick List: save candidates before experimenting with word order, replacements, capitalization, and punctuation.
✓

Private, local anagram generation

Your source phrase, filters, generated results, and Pick List remain in your browser. The Rust/WebAssembly search engine loads local word data and does not send the text you enter to a remote solving service.

Questions

Anagram solver FAQ

What is a phrase anagram?

A phrase anagram rearranges every letter from a name or phrase into new words. Spaces, punctuation, and capitalization do not affect the match.

Are all results exact?

Yes. Each displayed phrase is validated against the source and must contain exactly the same letters.

How are anagram results ranked?

The solver estimates readability and naturalness using word frequency, grammar, local phrase evidence, word order, and phrase shape. Exactness is guaranteed, but ranking is an estimate rather than a claim that the first result is always the cleverest.

How do grammar templates help find anagrams?

Templates constrain results to structures such as [Noun] of [Noun] and [Verb] the [Noun]. The solver automatically reserves literal connector words such as “of” or “the.”

Why do some searches take longer?

Long phrases, larger dictionaries, short minimum word lengths, and Exhaustive depth create far more combinations to explore. Worker progress and search throughput show what the local engine is doing.

How do I find a specific phrase?

Add its known structure to Phrase pattern or Grammar template, include confirmed words as Required, or mark promising vocabulary as Preferred.

What can I do in the Pick List phrase editor?

Open Edit to reorder words, freeze words in position, replace one word with an exact-letter alternative, apply capitalization and punctuation, preview the result, and copy the finished phrase.

What does the vulgar-word filter do?

It removes clearly explicit and profane dictionary entries before searching. It can be disabled under Advanced options.

Where is my Pick List stored?

The Pick List and its formatting choices are saved locally in this browser so they remain available across searches.

How long can an anagram phrase be?

The current solver accepts source phrases containing 2 to 30 letters. Support for longer phrases is planned for a future upgrade.
