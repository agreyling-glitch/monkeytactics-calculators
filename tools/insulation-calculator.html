How to use the Insulation Calculator

Enter the wall area in square feet and select the target R-value. Click Calculate Insulation to estimate the number of rolls or packs and their combined weight.

Measure only the cavities being insulated. Subtracting large doors and windows can improve accuracy, while leaving small openings included provides a modest waste allowance.

How is insulation quantity calculated?

Each R-value option uses representative package coverage. The wall area is divided by that coverage and rounded up because insulation is purchased in whole rolls or packs.

Rolls or packs = round up(wall area ÷ package coverage)
Total weight = rolls or packs × weight per package

Coverage and package weight vary by batt width, cavity spacing, thickness, and brand. Confirm the package label and local building-code requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What does insulation R-value mean?

    R-value measures resistance to heat flow. A higher R-value generally provides more thermal resistance when the insulation is installed correctly.

  • Should windows and doors be subtracted?

    Subtract large openings for a precise estimate. Keeping small openings in the area can help cover trimming and installation waste.

  • Can insulation be compressed to fit?

    Compressing fiberglass insulation reduces its effective R-value. Choose a product thickness designed for the cavity depth.
