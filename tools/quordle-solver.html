            02 / Play alongside Quordle

            How to Use the Quordle Solver

            1. Choose a dictionary: use Both for the broadest game coverage, ENABLE for a narrower North American list, or EXPANDED for British and North American vocabulary. Hover over or focus an option for details.
            2. Choose 4, 5, or 6 letters, then play a word of that length.
            3. Enter that shared guess in the solver.
            4. Select Board 1 through Board 4 and mark only the yellow and green tiles. Leave gray tiles unmarked.
            5. Select Add guess to all four boards to filter every possible-answer list.
            6. Review the gold Best shared next guess, then open its detailed per-board explanation to see entropy, feedback patterns, and expected candidates remaining.
            7. Select a result-board heading to expand its candidates and mirror that board’s colors on the keyboard.
            8. Mark solved boards complete so later recommendations focus on the boards that remain.

            Strategy

            How Entropy Improves a Quordle Guess

            Entropy estimates the information a guess is expected to reveal. A higher bit value means the resulting green, yellow, and gray patterns divide the remaining words into more useful groups.

            The solver calculates entropy independently for every unsolved board, then adds those values to rank a shared next guess. That favors words that can narrow several boards at once—not merely a likely answer for one board.

            Use the per-board bars to see where a guess is most informative. When a board is solved, mark it complete; the tool removes it from the ranking so later moves focus on unfinished boards.

            03 / Candidate control

            Focus on One Quordle Board

            Select any Board 1–4 result heading to expand that board and hide the other candidate lists. The expanded view shows more possible words of the selected length with less scrolling.

            The same board is outlined in every shared-guess history row, while its gray, yellow, and green letter states fill the on-screen keyboard. Select the heading again—or press Escape—to return to the four-board view.

            Word lists & privacy

            Choose the Right Dictionary

            Both is the recommended starting point because published games may accept words missing from one source list. Choose ENABLE for a narrower North American list or EXPANDED for British and North American word-game vocabulary. If a narrower list returns no candidates, the solver offers a one-click combined search.

            Candidate filtering, duplicate-letter scoring, entropy calculations, and guess ranking run locally in your browser. Your guesses are not sent to Merriam-Webster or used to look up the official daily answers.

            04 / Workspace controls

            Explore Candidates Your Way

            Use Board Layout to choose how many of the four feedback and analysis boards appear in each row. The layout is saved in this browser and automatically adapts on smaller screens.

            Expand any Board 1–4 analysis, then choose Alphabetical, Information, or Letter pattern. Alphabetical pages through every matching word, Information groups the strongest entropy choices, and Letter pattern organizes candidates by vowel and consonant structure.

            History & endgame

            Pause, Resume, and Finish

            Open the Shared guesses menu to save a reusable copy or restore the last copy saved in this browser. Each game is also saved automatically in local history. Use Edit beside any earlier guess to correct its word or board colors in place, or restore a saved game from Game history. The active history item receives a gold outline so you always know which game you are editing.

            After you add a shared guess, keyboard focus moves to Best shared next guess. When every unfinished board has only one candidate, a summary lists the remaining answers. Choose Continue marking letters—or press Escape—to close it and keep updating the boards.

            05 / Common questions

            Quordle Solver FAQ

            How do I use the Quordle solver?

            Choose 4, 5, or 6 letters, enter the guess you played, then mark only the yellow and green tiles on each board. Leave gray tiles unmarked; they count as absent when you add the guess and narrow all four answer lists.

            How does the next-guess recommendation work?

            The solver compares how well each guess divides the remaining candidates across every unsolved board and recommends the word with the strongest combined information score.

            What does entropy mean in the Quordle solver?

            Entropy estimates how much information a guess can reveal. A higher bit value means the guess is expected to split that board's remaining candidates into more useful groups.

            Can I expand one Quordle board?

            Yes. Select a result board heading to hide the other three boards and show a taller candidate list. The matching board is highlighted in shared-guess history, and its colors fill the keyboard. Select it again or press Escape to restore all four boards.

            Which dictionary should I choose?

            ENABLE is a broad North American English word list. EXPANDED adds British and North American word-game vocabulary. Choose Both for the widest candidate search.

            Does the solver handle repeated letters?

            Yes. Each board uses Wordle-style two-pass scoring, so gray, yellow, and green copies of a repeated letter create the correct minimum and maximum counts.

            Are guesses sent to Merriam-Webster?

            No. You copy the colored results into this independent tool, and its word filtering runs locally in your browser.
