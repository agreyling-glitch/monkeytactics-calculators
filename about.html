What MonkeyTactics stands for

Four principles that guide every tool we build.

🆓 Always Free

Every tool on this site is free to use — no paywalls, no subscriptions, no sign‑up required.

⚡ Fast & Lightweight

Pages load in milliseconds. Tools run instantly. No frameworks, no bloat — just clean HTML, CSS, and JS.

🔒 Private by Design

All calculations happen directly in your browser. Nothing you enter is ever sent to a server or stored anywhere.

✅ Accurate & Reliable

Each tool is carefully built and tested against known reference values so you can trust the results.

Our Mission

MonkeyTactics started from a simple frustration: too many calculator sites are slow, cluttered with ads, or demand you create an account just to run a basic calculation. We set out to fix that.

The goal is straightforward — build a growing library of tools that are genuinely useful, immediately accessible, and respectful of your time and privacy. Every page is hand‑crafted, tested on real devices, and designed to get out of your way as fast as possible.
